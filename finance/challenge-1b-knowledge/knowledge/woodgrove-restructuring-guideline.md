# Woodgrove Finance — Payment Plan and Restructuring Guideline

Document ID: WFL-GDL-RS-02 · Version 2.0 · Owner: Retail Credit Risk · Effective: 1 April 2026

> Fictional document for a Microsoft Foundry workshop. Woodgrove Finance is not a real company.
> This document is not legal or regulatory advice.

## 1. Purpose

A payment plan helps a borrower with a temporary loss of income to keep paying, instead of falling further behind. This guideline sets who is eligible, which options exist and who approves them.

## 2. Eligibility

All five conditions must be true:

| # | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| E1 | `days_past_due` is **60 or less** on the date of the request. |
| E2 | The borrower has a documented loss or delay of income, for example a business slowdown, late payments from a large buyer, a job loss or a medical emergency. |
| E3 | The borrower has paid **at least 6 EMIs** since disbursement. |
| E4 | The loan has **not been restructured in the last 24 months**. |
| E5 | The account has no fraud flag. |

If any condition is false, the account stays in the normal collections procedure (WFL-POL-COL-07).

## 3. Options

| Option | What changes | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **RS-A Tenure extension** | The tenure grows by up to 24 months, so the EMI falls. | Income has fallen for the long term but is stable. |
| **RS-B Step-up EMI** | The borrower pays 50% of the EMI for up to 6 months. The EMI then steps up to recover the gap within the remaining tenure. | Income is delayed or seasonal and is expected to recover, for example a business waiting for buyer payments. |
| **RS-C Moratorium** | No EMI for up to 2 months. Interest continues to accrue. | Only for a documented medical emergency or a natural disaster. |

Choose one option. Do not combine options.

## 4. Documents

1. Form **RS-1** (payment plan request), signed by the borrower and every co-borrower.
2. Bank statements for the last 6 months.
3. For business loans: the last 2 GST returns.
4. Proof of the income event, for example buyer payment records, a termination letter or a hospital bill.

## 5. Approval

| Outstanding amount | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to ₹25,00,000 | Retail credit risk manager |
| Above ₹25,00,000 | Credit risk committee |

The collections officer prepares the case. The approver decides. Staff and AI assistants must never tell a borrower that a plan is approved before the approver decides.

## 6. After approval

1. Flag the account "restructured" for 12 months.
2. Do not offer a top-up loan or new credit for 12 months.
3. Report the account to the credit bureaus as the regulations require.
4. Review the account each month. If two EMIs under the plan bounce, end the plan and return the account to COL-PRC-05.

## 7. Turnaround

The approver decides within 5 working days of receiving a complete Form RS-1.

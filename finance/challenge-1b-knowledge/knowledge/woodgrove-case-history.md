# Woodgrove Finance — Collections Case History (sample)

Document ID: WFL-REC-COL-2026 · Owner: Retail Collections Quality

> Fictional and pseudonymised cases for a Microsoft Foundry workshop. Woodgrove Finance is not a real company.
> Cases are pseudonymised: they contain no names or ID numbers.

## CASE-2025-118 — Unsecured business loan, late buyer payments

- **Signals before default:** new credit enquiries rose above 3 about three months before the first unpaid EMI. EMI bounces and a bureau score drop of more than 40 points followed. The account became 52 days past due.
- **Reason:** a large buyer paid the borrower's invoices 90 days late.
- **Action:** COL-PRC-05 priority resolution. The borrower was eligible under WFL-GDL-RS-02. The retail credit risk manager approved option **RS-B (step-up EMI)** for 6 months.
- **Outcome:** the account returned to 0 days past due after 5 months.
- **Lesson:** the rise in credit enquiries was the earliest signal. An early outreach call at that point would have reduced the loss.

## CASE-2025-131 — Personal loan, borrowing from several lenders

- **Signals:** bureau score fell 70 points in 90 days and there were 7 new credit enquiries. No days past due.
- **Reason:** the borrower took three small digital loans to pay for a family event.
- **Action:** COL-PRC-03 early outreach call within 7 days. The branch did not offer a top-up loan. The borrower moved the EMI date to two days after salary credit.
- **Outcome:** no EMI was missed.
- **Lesson:** an early call works for this pattern. A top-up loan would have added to the borrower's debt.

## CASE-2026-022 — Loan against property, payment mandate problem

- **Signals:** two EMI bounces in two months and 18 days past due. The bureau score was stable.
- **Reason:** the borrower moved his salary to a new bank. The NACH mandate still pointed to the old account.
- **Action:** COL-PRC-02 mandate check. The branch re-registered the mandate.
- **Outcome:** paid in full 9 days later.
- **Lesson:** check the mandate before you treat bounces as financial stress.

## CASE-2026-047 — Complaint about a collection agency

- **What happened:** an agency called a borrower's employer about an overdue EMI.
- **Finding:** the complaint was upheld. This broke conduct rule 2 of WFL-POL-COL-07.
- **Action:** the agency was suspended for 60 days and retrained. The borrower received a written apology.
- **Lesson:** never discuss a loan with anyone who is not on the loan.

## CASE-2026-063 — Used car loan, one bounce

- **Signals:** one EMI bounce and 4 days past due. All other signals within limits.
- **Action:** COL-PRC-01 payment reminder (TPL-REM-01).
- **Outcome:** paid 2 days later.
- **Lesson:** a reminder is enough for a single late payment. A call would have added cost and annoyed the borrower.

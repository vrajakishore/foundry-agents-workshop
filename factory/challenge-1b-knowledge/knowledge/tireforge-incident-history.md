# TireForge Industries — Incident History, Production Line A

Document ID: TF-IH-LA. Period: January 2025 to April 2026. Owner: Reliability Engineering.

This history is fictional. It supports the Microsoft Foundry workshop labs.

## INC-2025-014 — CP-003 Curing Press

- Date: 2025-06-11
- Readings at alarm: temperature 191 °C, pressure 17.5 bar, vibration 6.8 mm/s, speed 0 rpm.
- Root cause: pressure relief valve PRV-3 stuck closed. Two platen mounting bolts were loose. The bladder seal leaked steam.
- Fix: procedure CP-PRC-07. New valve TF-CP-PRV3 and seal kit TF-CP-SK12. Bolts torqued to 320 N·m.
- Downtime: 9 hours. 46 tyres held for inspection. 11 tyres scrapped.
- Lesson: the operator waited for the cure cycle to end. The leak became worse. Stop the press at once when two or more readings are above the limit.

## INC-2025-031 — MX-001 Mixer

- Date: 2025-09-02
- Readings at alarm: temperature 94 °C, vibration 5.1 mm/s.
- Root cause: rotor bearing had too little grease. The grease interval was missed by 180 hours.
- Fix: procedure MX-PRC-03. Bearing TF-MX-B220 replaced.
- Downtime: 4 hours.
- Lesson: the bearing temperature rose for three days before the alarm. Watch the trend, not only the limit.

## INC-2026-002 — EX-002 Extruder

- Date: 2026-01-19
- Readings at alarm: temperature 133 °C, pressure 15.9 bar.
- Root cause: clogged screen pack.
- Fix: procedure EX-PRC-02. Screen pack TF-EX-SP40 replaced.
- Downtime: 1.5 hours.

## INC-2026-009 — IS-005 Inspection Station

- Date: 2026-02-23
- Readings at alarm: vibration 5.0 mm/s.
- Root cause: false alarm. The vibration sensor mount was loose after a cleaning shift.
- Fix: mount tightened. Station recalibrated.
- Downtime: 30 minutes.
- Lesson: check the sensor mount before you replace the spindle bearing.

## INC-2026-017 — CU-004 Cooling Unit

- Date: 2026-04-02
- Readings at alarm: temperature 47 °C, pressure 0.7 bar, speed 76 rpm.
- Root cause: worn coolant pump impeller.
- Fix: procedure CU-PRC-01. Impeller TF-CU-P15 replaced.
- Downtime: 3 hours. 28 tyres held for inspection. None scrapped.

# TireForge Industries — Maintenance Policy

Document ID: TF-POL-07. Owner: Plant Manager.

This policy is fictional. It supports the Microsoft Foundry workshop labs.

## Urgency levels

| Urgency | When to use it | Response time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IMMEDIATE | Two or more readings are above the limit, or any curing press reading is above the limit | Stop the machine now |
| WITHIN 24H | One reading is above the limit | Start the procedure within 24 hours |
| MONITOR | All readings are inside the limits, but one reading is within 5% of a limit | Check the trend at the next shift |

## Work orders

1. Open a work order for every maintenance procedure.
2. A shift supervisor must approve each work order before work starts.
3. An AI agent can propose a work order. An AI agent must not open a work order without approval.
4. Write the procedure ID (for example, CP-PRC-07) in the work order.

## Safety

1. Apply lockout/tagout before you touch a machine.
2. Release all steam and hydraulic pressure before you open a curing press.
3. Never bypass a pressure relief valve.

## Quality holds

Hold all tyres for quality inspection when:

- a curing press stops during a cure cycle, or
- the cooling unit runs outside its limits, or
- the inspection station vibration is above 4.0 mm/s.

## Escalation

| Situation | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| IMMEDIATE urgency | Shift supervisor and on-call reliability engineer |
| Same fault twice in 30 days | Reliability Engineering lead |
| Any injury or near miss | Plant safety officer |
